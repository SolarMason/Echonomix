Frequently Asked Questions

Everything to know about Echonomix.

Quick, conversational answers about the Smart-Board, the live Coinbase market, and crypto staking. Optimized for voice assistants and AI search.

What is the Echonomix Smart-Board?

The Echonomix Founder's Edition Smart-Board is the world's first NFC-Ready learning blockchain board game. It uses NFC technology so any phone or tablet can tap the board, cards, or tokens to unlock educational lessons about Bitcoin, Ethereum, staking, and Web3.

When is the Echonomix board game launching?

Echonomix is pre-launch. The Founder's Edition Smart-Board is coming soon. Sales channels are paused while production is being finalized. Email noel@solarmason.com to be notified at launch.

Where is Echonomix Inc. located?

Echonomix Inc. is based in Northeast Pennsylvania, in the Scranton, PA region of the United States.

Who founded Echonomix?

Echonomix was founded by Noel Segui — a U.S. veteran and operating engineer based in Clarks Summit, PA. He began as a Bitcoin miner in a Scranton loft, sold Bitmain Antminer S9 mining hardware on eBay, and pivoted to long-term crypto investing and educational product development.

Is the Echonomix Smart-Board patented?

Yes. The Founder's Edition Smart-Board is Patent Pending.

How does NFC work on the Smart-Board?

Each space, card, and token on the board contains an NFC tag. When a smartphone or tablet with NFC enabled is tapped against the tag, the device opens the corresponding educational content — videos, walkthroughs, and lessons about that crypto concept.

What can I learn from the Smart-Board?

The board teaches blockchain fundamentals, Bitcoin denomination and halving cycles, Ethereum and Web 3.0, staking and DeFi yield, hardware-wallet security, and the difference between mining, staking, and investing.

What is the highest crypto staking APY on Coinbase right now?

Cosmos (ATOM) currently offers the highest published staking APY on Coinbase at approximately 12.00%, with a 21-day unbonding period. Polkadot (DOT) follows at approximately 9.50% with a 28-day lock-up. Rates float with network conditions and Coinbase's trailing-window calculation. See the live staking band →

Can I stake Bitcoin on Coinbase?

No. Bitcoin uses Proof-of-Work, not Proof-of-Stake, so it cannot be staked. Coinbase staking is available for Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), Cardano (ADA), Cosmos (ATOM), Tezos (XTZ), Polkadot (DOT), and Algorand (ALGO).

When is the next Bitcoin halving?

The next Bitcoin halving is estimated for April 2028 at block height 1,050,000. The block reward will decrease from 3.125 BTC to 1.5625 BTC. See the live countdown →

Where can I buy crypto?

Echonomix recommends Coinbase for beginners, Coinbase Advanced for active traders, and Coinbase Earn for passive staking rewards.

What hardware wallets does Echonomix recommend?

For long-term cold storage, Echonomix recommends the SafePal S1 (air-gapped, camera-only), the Ellipal Titan (full metal sealed), or the Ledger Nano X (Bluetooth-enabled with secure element).
